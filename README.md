# Experiment 1 — Study of Microwave Components and Instruments

---

## Aim

To become familiar with the microwave components and instruments available in the laboratory.

## Apparatus Used

Klystron power supply, Gunn power supply, VSWR meter, power meter, slotted section, frequency/wave meter, RF generator, Vector Network Analyzer.

**Components / devices:** attenuator, circulator, isolator, waveguide twist, magic tee, E-plane and H-plane tee, directional coupler, matched termination, PIN modulator, crystal detector, reflex klystron tube, Gunn diode, and the different antennas available.

## Introduction

A microwave test bench is an assembly of microwave components held together by nuts and bolts. A microwave source (oscillator) sits at one end; the generated waves travel down a waveguide through the various components so that the propagation of the waves and their interaction/processing by each component can be observed.

## 1. Klystron Power Supply

<img width="271" height="215" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3f1f0c79-da13-4824-a32c-5945c0f3e361" />

A regulated supply for operating low-power klystrons. It generates the voltages needed to drive reflex klystron tubes such as the 2K25, 2K56 and 2K22. The supply is stable, regulated and short-circuit protected, allows the beam voltage to be varied continuously, and has built-in square-wave and saw-tooth generators for amplitude and frequency modulation.

## 2. Reflex Klystron (Klystron Mount with Tube)

<img width="218" height="223" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/88a6c65e-7df5-4737-963a-3e07f78e56b3" />

A waveguide section of suitable length carrying an octal base on its broad wall for mounting the klystron tube. A movable short at one end directs the microwave energy generated by the tube. A small hole at the exact centre of the broad wall takes the coupling pin of the tube, since the electric field vector of the EM energy is maximum at the centre. Maximum power transfer is obtained by tuning the movable plunger.
